I’ve struggled to write a blog for a long time. I’ve always wanted to write, to put my thoughts on paper. Every time I’ve tried, though, I’d have to sit down, commit, and do some manual editing to get to a place where I felt proud of it. That was too much effort and with my constant travel schedule, I never got around to really writing out blog posts.
I’ve used Notion for a few years now, to keep track of things or ideas that I should be looking into. It’s convenient — everything’s in Markdown, there’s an iOS app, I can write wherever I am without having to lug my laptop and commit my posts.
In Atomic Habits by James Clear, Clear makes it clear (hah) that one of the fundamental ways to build a habit is to make it easy. The barrier to entry for writing on Notion is really low — just write on your phone, then move the Notion page when you feel like it’s done. Yeah, the first stuff might be bad, but it’ll get better.
That’s what I’m hoping to do here — just have a consistent place to put my thoughts on tech, politics, economics, and triathlon. I read a lot so hopefully you can extract some value as well.
